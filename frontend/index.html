Limitations

  • Only 30 dates × 2 units and 3 outage rows: estimates are noisy; intervals are indicative.
  • Units A/B are inferred from row order (no site id in the file). Weather is observed, not forecast, so weather-driven misses cannot be separated from forecast-input error.
  • Attribution is linear and associational, not causal. “Unexplained” means no available driver accounts for it.
